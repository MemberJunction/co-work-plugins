# /bylaws-review

Conduct comprehensive bylaws review and modernization.

## Usage

```
/bylaws-review [review-type] [options]
```

## Arguments

- `review-type`: Type of review (comprehensive, targeted, compliance-check, modernization)
- `--current-bylaws`: Path to current bylaws document or key provisions to review
- `--state`: State of incorporation for legal compliance
- `--issues`: Specific issues or sections to address
- `--org-type`: Organization type (501c3, 501c6, professional society, trade association)

## What This Command Does

Guide comprehensive bylaws review and improvement:

### 1. Bylaws Assessment Framework

**Compliance Check**
- State nonprofit corporation act compliance
- IRS requirements for tax-exempt status
- Industry-specific regulations
- Conflict with articles of incorporation

**Functionality Review**
- Operational pain points
- Governance bottlenecks
- Outdated provisions
- Missing provisions
- Ambiguous language

**Best Practice Comparison**
- Modern governance standards
- Peer organization practices
- ASAE recommendations
- Legal developments

### 2. Standard Bylaws Sections

**Article I: Name and Purpose**
- Legal name accuracy
- Purpose statement alignment with IRS
- Mission connection

**Article II: Members**
- Membership classes and qualifications
- Application and admission process
- Rights and privileges by class
- Dues and assessments
- Good standing requirements
- Discipline and termination
- Resignation procedures
- Voting rights allocation

**Article III: Meetings of Members**
- Annual meeting requirements
- Special meeting procedures
- Notice requirements (timing, method)
- Quorum requirements
- Voting procedures
- Proxy voting (if permitted)
- Electronic meeting provisions
- Action without meeting

**Article IV: Board of Directors**
- Powers and responsibilities
- Number and composition
- Qualifications
- Election/selection process
- Terms and term limits
- Vacancy filling
- Removal procedures
- Compensation policy

**Article V: Board Meetings**
- Regular meeting schedule
- Special meeting procedures
- Notice requirements
- Quorum requirements
- Voting and decision-making
- Electronic participation
- Executive session provisions
- Action without meeting

**Article VI: Officers**
- Officer positions
- Qualifications
- Election/appointment process
- Terms of office
- Duties and responsibilities
- Vacancy procedures
- Removal process

**Article VII: Committees**
- Standing committees
- Executive committee scope
- Committee creation authority
- Committee powers and limitations
- Reporting requirements

**Article VIII: Executive Director/CEO**
- Authority and responsibilities
- Relationship to board
- Evaluation provisions

**Article IX: Financial Provisions**
- Fiscal year
- Budget approval
- Audit requirements
- Signing authority
- Investment policy reference
- Reserve requirements

**Article X: Indemnification**
- Scope of indemnification
- Advancement of expenses
- Insurance provisions

**Article XI: Amendments**
- Proposal process
- Notice requirements
- Approval thresholds
- Member vote requirements

**Article XII: Dissolution**
- Dissolution procedures
- Asset distribution
- State compliance

### 3. Common Issues & Modernization

**Governance Flexibility**
- Virtual meeting authorization
- Electronic voting provisions
- Written consent procedures
- Emergency provisions

**Board Composition**
- Diversity requirements
- Skills-based recruitment
- Term limits implementation
- Ex-officio clarification

**Membership Evolution**
- New membership categories
- Tiered voting rights
- Dues flexibility
- Corporate membership structure

**Decision-Making**
- Quorum thresholds (often too high)
- Supermajority requirements
- Committee authority clarification
- Delegation provisions

**Compliance Updates**
- State law changes
- IRS requirements
- Conflict of interest provisions
- Document retention references

### 4. Review Process Guide

**Phase 1: Assessment**
- Gather current governing documents
- Identify stakeholder concerns
- Review operational issues
- Research legal requirements
- Benchmark peer organizations

**Phase 2: Analysis**
- Section-by-section review
- Gap identification
- Conflict resolution
- Priority ranking

**Phase 3: Drafting**
- Proposed revisions
- Redline comparison
- Legal review
- Staff input

**Phase 4: Approval**
- Board review and recommendation
- Member notice requirements
- Membership vote
- Documentation and filing

### 5. Redline Document Generation

Create comparison document showing:
- Deleted language (strikethrough)
- New language (underline)
- Explanatory notes for each change
- Rationale summary

### 6. Member Communication

**Pre-Vote Materials**
- Summary of proposed changes
- Rationale document
- FAQ
- Comparison chart
- Timeline

**Voting Materials**
- Ballot design
- Voting instructions
- Deadline reminders
- Quorum tracking

### 7. Legal Considerations

**When to Involve Legal Counsel**
- State law compliance
- Tax-exempt status implications
- Indemnification provisions
- Dissolution language
- Complex structural changes

**State-Specific Requirements**
- Notice timing and method
- Vote thresholds
- Filing requirements
- Member approval requirements

## Best Practices Applied

- Regular review cycle (every 3-5 years)
- Comprehensive rather than piecemeal changes
- Member education before voting
- Legal review for significant changes
- Clear implementation timeline
- Policy separation from bylaws
- Flexibility for operational matters
- Compliance verification

## Output Formats

- Assessment report
- Redline revision document
- Summary of changes
- Member communication package
- Board presentation
- Voting materials

## Example

```
/bylaws-review comprehensive --state="Delaware" --org-type="501c6" --issues="virtual meetings, board term limits, membership categories"
```

Conducts a comprehensive bylaws review for a Delaware 501(c)(6) organization with focus on modernizing virtual meeting provisions, implementing board term limits, and restructuring membership categories.
