# /grant-proposal

Develop compelling grant proposals and funding applications.

## Usage

```
/grant-proposal [grant-type] [options]
```

## Arguments

- `grant-type`: Type of funding (foundation, government, corporate, research, capacity-building)
- `--funder`: Specific funder or program if known
- `--amount`: Requested funding amount
- `--project`: Brief project description
- `--timeline`: Project duration

## What This Command Does

Generate comprehensive grant proposal materials:

### 1. Funder Research & Alignment

**Funder Profile Analysis**
- Mission and priorities alignment
- Funding history and patterns
- Average grant size
- Geographic focus
- Application requirements and deadlines

**Alignment Assessment**
- How project fits funder priorities
- Previous relationships
- Competition landscape
- Success probability factors

### 2. Proposal Narrative Structure

**Executive Summary**
- Organization overview (1 paragraph)
- Problem statement (1 paragraph)
- Proposed solution (1 paragraph)
- Request and impact (1 paragraph)

**Statement of Need**
- Problem definition with data
- Who is affected and how
- Root causes
- Why now (urgency)
- Gap in current solutions
- Your unique position to address

**Project Description**

*Goals and Objectives*
- SMART goal statements
- Measurable objectives
- Alignment with funder priorities

*Methods and Approach*
- Program design rationale
- Activities and interventions
- Evidence base/best practices
- Innovation elements
- Timeline with milestones

*Target Population*
- Who will be served
- How they'll be reached
- Inclusion and accessibility
- Cultural competency

*Partnerships*
- Collaborating organizations
- Roles and contributions
- Letters of support needs

**Evaluation Plan**
- Process evaluation components
- Outcome measures
- Data collection methods
- Analysis approach
- Reporting plan
- Learning and adaptation

**Sustainability Plan**
- Post-grant continuation strategy
- Revenue diversification
- Capacity building
- Institutionalization

**Organizational Capacity**
- Mission and history
- Relevant experience
- Staff qualifications
- Board governance
- Financial health
- Past successes

### 3. Budget Development

**Direct Costs**
- Personnel (salaries, benefits, % effort)
- Consultants and contractors
- Equipment and supplies
- Travel
- Participant support
- Other direct costs

**Indirect Costs**
- Rate calculation or negotiation
- Allowable vs. restricted costs

**Budget Narrative**
- Line-item justifications
- Cost reasonableness
- In-kind contributions
- Matching funds

**Multi-Year Projections** (if applicable)
- Annual breakdowns
- Inflation assumptions
- Scaling considerations

### 4. Supporting Documents Checklist

Standard attachments:
- 501(c)(3) determination letter
- Board list with affiliations
- Organizational chart
- Key staff resumes
- Financial statements (audited if available)
- Annual report
- Letters of support/commitment
- Logic model/theory of change
- Memoranda of understanding

### 5. Logic Model/Theory of Change

Visual framework showing:
- Inputs (resources invested)
- Activities (what you'll do)
- Outputs (direct products)
- Short-term outcomes
- Long-term outcomes
- Impact

### 6. Cover Letter

Professional cover letter including:
- Specific ask amount and purpose
- Brief organizational credentials
- Connection to funder's mission
- Key differentiators
- Contact information
- Gratitude and next steps

### 7. Common Funder Types

**Foundation Grants**
- Focus on mission alignment
- Relationship building emphasis
- Multi-year potential
- Reporting requirements

**Government Grants**
- Strict compliance requirements
- Detailed budgets and forms
- Audit considerations
- Matching requirements

**Corporate Funding**
- Brand alignment
- Recognition opportunities
- Employee engagement ties
- Shorter timelines

## Best Practices Applied

- Start with funder research
- Tell a compelling story with data
- Be specific and realistic
- Show organizational capacity
- Demonstrate sustainability thinking
- Follow instructions exactly
- Allow time for internal review
- Build relationships before asking
- Budget accurately (don't lowball)

## Output Formats

- Full narrative document
- Budget spreadsheet template
- Logic model graphic
- Executive summary standalone
- Funder-specific formats

## Example

```
/grant-proposal foundation --funder="Community Foundation" --amount="$75,000" --project="Member professional development scholarship program" --timeline="24 months"
```

Creates a foundation grant proposal for a two-year scholarship program.
