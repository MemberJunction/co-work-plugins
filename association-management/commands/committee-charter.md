# /committee-charter

Create and maintain committee, task force, and working group charters.

## Usage

```
/committee-charter [charter-type] [options]
```

## Arguments

- `charter-type`: Type of group (standing-committee, task-force, working-group, advisory-council, sig)
- `--name`: Committee or group name
- `--purpose`: Primary purpose/focus area
- `--duration`: Ongoing or time-limited (with end date)
- `--scope`: Scope of authority

## What This Command Does

Generate comprehensive committee governance documents:

### 1. Charter Document Structure

**Committee Identification**
- Official name
- Abbreviation (if applicable)
- Type (standing, ad hoc, task force, etc.)
- Parent body/reporting relationship
- Date established
- Charter approval date
- Review cycle

**Purpose Statement**
- Clear, concise purpose (2-3 sentences)
- How it supports association mission
- Problems it addresses
- Value it provides

**Scope & Authority**
- Decision-making authority level:
  - Recommending only
  - Limited decision authority
  - Full authority in specific areas
- Budget authority (if any)
- Staff direction authority (if any)
- What is explicitly out of scope

**Responsibilities & Functions**

*Core Functions*
- Primary ongoing responsibilities
- Regular deliverables
- Reporting requirements
- Board support functions

*Specific Tasks*
- Annual priorities
- Project assignments
- Review responsibilities
- Advisory functions

**Membership**

*Composition*
- Number of members (range or specific)
- Required representation (geographic, sector, etc.)
- Ex-officio members
- Staff liaison designation
- Board liaison (if applicable)

*Qualifications*
- Required expertise/experience
- Membership status requirements
- Conflict of interest considerations
- Diversity considerations

*Selection Process*
- Nomination procedure
- Appointment authority
- Application requirements
- Interview process (if applicable)

*Terms*
- Length of term
- Term limits
- Staggered terms structure
- Vacancy procedures
- Removal process

**Leadership**

*Chair/Vice Chair*
- Selection method
- Term of leadership
- Responsibilities
- Succession planning

*Leadership Responsibilities*
- Meeting facilitation
- Agenda development
- Member engagement
- Board reporting
- Work plan oversight

**Operations**

*Meetings*
- Frequency (minimum and typical)
- Format (in-person, virtual, hybrid)
- Quorum requirements
- Notice requirements
- Attendance expectations

*Decision Making*
- Voting procedures
- Consensus vs. majority
- Proxy/absentee voting
- Documentation requirements

*Communication*
- Internal communication methods
- Confidentiality expectations
- External communication protocols
- Social media guidelines

**Reporting & Accountability**
- Reporting frequency
- Report format/template
- Recipient (board, staff, members)
- Annual work plan requirement
- Performance evaluation

**Resources & Support**
- Staff support provided
- Budget allocation (if any)
- Technology tools
- Travel reimbursement policy
- Meeting expenses

**Relationship to Other Bodies**
- Coordination with other committees
- Subcommittee authority
- Working group creation authority
- Board interaction protocols

**Charter Maintenance**
- Amendment process
- Review frequency
- Sunset provisions (for task forces)
- Dissolution procedures

### 2. Supporting Documents

**Application Form**
- Personal information
- Qualifications summary
- Statement of interest
- Time commitment acknowledgment
- Conflict of interest disclosure

**Member Agreement**
- Expectations acknowledgment
- Confidentiality agreement
- Conflict of interest policy
- Code of conduct acceptance

**Annual Work Plan Template**
- Goals and objectives
- Key initiatives
- Timeline/milestones
- Resource needs
- Success metrics

**Meeting Agenda Template**
- Standard agenda items
- Time allocations
- Preparation requirements
- Follow-up structure

**Report Template**
- Activities summary
- Progress on goals
- Decisions made
- Recommendations
- Upcoming priorities

### 3. Committee Types

**Standing Committee**
- Ongoing, permanent
- Defined in bylaws
- Core governance function
- Examples: Executive, Finance, Nominating, Ethics

**Special/Ad Hoc Committee**
- Specific purpose
- Time-limited
- Sunsets upon completion
- Examples: Search, Anniversary, Policy Review

**Task Force**
- Focused assignment
- Short-term (typically 6-18 months)
- Action-oriented
- Dissolves when task complete

**Working Group**
- Informal structure
- Staff or committee-led
- Operational focus
- Flexible membership

**Advisory Council**
- External expertise
- Non-governing
- Input and guidance
- May include non-members

**Special Interest Group (SIG)**
- Member-driven
- Interest-based
- Self-organizing
- Lower governance requirements

### 4. Governance Best Practices

**Effective Committee Design**
- Right-sized membership (7-15 typical)
- Clear purpose and value
- Appropriate authority level
- Diverse perspectives
- Strong leadership
- Adequate support

**Common Pitfalls to Avoid**
- Mission creep
- Rubber-stamp mentality
- Poor attendance tolerance
- Inadequate staff support
- No term limits
- Unclear decision authority

**Engagement Strategies**
- Meaningful work assignments
- Clear expectations
- Regular communication
- Recognition and appreciation
- Development opportunities

## Output Formats

- Complete charter document
- Supporting form templates
- Committee handbook section
- Board presentation summary

## Example

```
/committee-charter standing-committee --name="Professional Development Committee" --purpose="Oversee education and certification programs" --duration=ongoing --scope="recommending to board with operational authority for program content"
```

Creates a charter for a standing professional development committee with appropriate governance structure.
