# /annual-report

Create compelling annual reports and impact summaries.

## Usage

```
/annual-report [report-type] [options]
```

## Arguments

- `report-type`: Type of report (full-annual, impact-summary, financial-highlights, member-report)
- `--year`: Reporting year
- `--audience`: Primary audience (members, board, funders, public)
- `--format`: Print, digital, interactive, or all
- `--theme`: Optional thematic focus

## What This Command Does

Generate comprehensive annual report content:

### 1. Report Structure

**Cover & Introduction**
- Compelling cover concept
- Letter from Board Chair
- Letter from CEO/Executive Director
- Mission reaffirmation
- Year at a glance highlights

**Mission & Impact**
- Mission statement
- Vision and values
- Theory of change
- Strategic goals progress
- Key achievements

**By the Numbers**
- Infographic-ready statistics
- Year-over-year comparisons
- Benchmark context
- Visual data presentation

**Program Highlights**
- Major initiatives
- New programs launched
- Program outcomes with metrics
- Success stories
- Member testimonials

**Advocacy & Industry Impact**
- Policy wins
- Regulatory engagement
- Industry leadership
- Public awareness efforts
- Coalition work

**Events & Education**
- Conference/event recap
- Educational program summary
- Attendance and satisfaction metrics
- Speaker highlights
- Upcoming initiatives

**Membership**
- Growth/retention metrics
- Member demographics
- Engagement highlights
- New member recruitment
- Member satisfaction data

**Governance & Leadership**
- Board of Directors listing
- Committee highlights
- Leadership transitions
- Governance improvements
- Strategic planning updates

**Financial Summary**
- Statement of financial position
- Statement of activities
- Revenue breakdown (pie chart)
- Expense allocation
- Key financial ratios
- Auditor information

**Recognition**
- Award recipients
- Volunteer recognition
- Sponsor/donor acknowledgment
- Partner appreciation
- Staff recognition

**Looking Ahead**
- Next year priorities
- Upcoming initiatives
- Call to action
- How to get involved

### 2. Content Development

**Impact Stories**
- Member success narratives
- Beneficiary testimonials
- Industry transformation examples

**Data Visualization**

- Geographic maps
- Timeline visuals

**Photography & Imagery**
- Event photography guidance
- Member portrait suggestions
- Infographic concepts
- Icon and illustration needs

- How dues were invested

**Board Version**
- Governance focus
- Strategic progress

**Funder/Donor Version**
- Impact metrics
- Stewardship reporting
- Outcome documentation

**Public/Media Version**
- Industry impact
- Public benefit
- Thought leadership positioning
- Press-ready content

**Print Publication**
- Professional layout guidelines
- Paper and printing specifications
- Distribution planning
- Cost considerations

**Digital PDF**
- Interactive elements
- Hyperlinked content

**Interactive Web**
- Scrolling narrative

- Issues engaged
- Legislative wins

**Financial**

## Best Practices Applied

- Lead with impact, not activities
- Show, don't just tell (data + stories)
- Design for skimmability
- Maintain brand consistency
- Make it accessible (WCAG compliance)
- Include clear calls to action
- Acknowledge all contributors
- Balance transparency with readability

## Output Formats

- Full report draft (Word/Google Docs)
- Design brief for layout
- Data visualization specifications
- Executive summary version
- Web content structure

## Example

```
/annual-report full-annual --year=2024 --audience=members --format=all --theme="Building Tomorrow Together"
```

Creates a comprehensive annual report with print, digital, and interactive versions themed around forward-looking progress.
