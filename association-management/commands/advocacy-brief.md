# /advocacy-brief

Create compelling advocacy briefs, position papers, and policy documents.

## Usage

```
/advocacy-brief [document-type] [options]
```

## Arguments

- `document-type`: Type of document (position-paper, issue-brief, talking-points, testimony, comment-letter, fact-sheet, one-pager)
- `--issue`: The policy issue or legislation
- `--audience`: Target audience (legislators, regulators, media, members, coalition)
- `--jurisdiction`: Federal, state, local, or international
- `--stance`: Support, oppose, amend, or neutral/informational

## What This Command Does

Generate professional advocacy documents:

### 1. Issue Analysis Framework

**Issue Overview**
- Clear, jargon-free explanation of the issue
- Current status (pending legislation, proposed rule, etc.)
- Timeline and key dates
- Relevant regulatory/legislative bodies

**Stakeholder Mapping**
- Champions and allies
- Opposition and critics
- Undecided/persuadable targets
- Coalition partners

**Impact Assessment**
- Impact on association members
- Economic implications (quantified when possible)
- Workforce effects
- Consumer/public impact
- Unintended consequences

### 2. Position Paper Structure

**Executive Summary**
- Association's position (1-2 sentences)
- Top 3 supporting arguments
- Requested action

**Background**
- Issue history and context
- Current landscape
- Why this matters now

**Association Position**
- Clear statement of stance
- Rationale and justification
- Alignment with mission/values

**Supporting Arguments**
- Argument 1 with evidence
- Argument 2 with evidence
- Argument 3 with evidence
- Anticipate and address counterarguments

**Recommendations**
- Specific policy recommendations
- Alternative approaches if applicable
- Implementation considerations

**Call to Action**
- What decision-makers should do
- Timeline for action
- Contact information

### 3. One-Pager/Fact Sheet

Condensed format for quick consumption:
- Issue headline
- 3-4 key facts with citations
- Association position
- Member impact (with real examples)
- Ask/recommendation
- Contact information

### 4. Talking Points

For member advocates and spokespeople:

**Core Message** (30-second version)
- The hook
- The problem
- The solution
- The ask

**Supporting Points**
- Data and statistics
- Member stories (anonymized)
- Economic arguments
- Comparison to other jurisdictions

**Anticipated Questions**
- Likely challenges with responses
- Bridge phrases to stay on message
- Topics to avoid and why

**Do's and Don'ts**
- Effective framing
- Language to use/avoid
- Tone guidance

### 5. Legislative Testimony

Formal testimony structure:
- Introduction and credentials
- Association overview and standing
- Position statement
- Supporting evidence (3-5 key points)
- Member impact stories
- Recommendations
- Closing and availability for questions

### 6. Regulatory Comment Letter

Formal comment structure:
- Proper addressee and docket reference
- Standing and interest declaration
- Summary of comments
- Section-by-section analysis
- Specific recommended changes
- Supporting data and research
- Conclusion and contact

### 7. Research Integration

When using search capabilities:
- Current bill status and text
- Recent amendments or developments
- Opponent arguments and responses
- Comparable legislation in other jurisdictions
- Relevant studies and data
- Media coverage and public sentiment

## Best Practices Applied

- Lead with impact, not process
- Use concrete examples and data
- Keep asks specific and actionable
- Maintain nonpartisan framing
- Cite credible sources
- Include member voice/stories
- Accessible language (avoid jargon)
- Professional, respectful tone
- Proper disclosure and registration compliance

## Output Formats

- Word document for editing
- PDF for distribution
- Web content for advocacy center
- Social media summary
- Email template for grassroots

## Example

```
/advocacy-brief issue-brief --issue="Proposed licensing requirements for [profession]" --audience=legislators --jurisdiction=state --stance=amend
```

Creates an issue brief opposing portions of proposed state licensing requirements with specific amendment recommendations.
