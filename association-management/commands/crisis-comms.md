# /crisis-comms

Develop crisis communication plans and manage urgent communications.

## Usage

```
/crisis-comms [mode] [options]
```

## Arguments

- `mode`: Operating mode (plan-development, active-response, post-crisis, simulation)
- `--crisis-type`: Type of crisis (reputational, operational, industry, leadership, financial, safety, cyber)
- `--severity`: Crisis severity level (1-low to 5-critical)
- `--stakeholders`: Primary affected stakeholders
- `--timeline`: Response timeline or plan horizon

## What This Command Does

Prepare for and manage crisis communications:

**Plan Structure**

*Purpose and Scope*
- Plan objectives
- Crisis definition
- Activation criteria
- Plan ownership

*Crisis Classification*
- Severity levels (1-5)
- Crisis categories
- Escalation triggers
- Response protocols by level

*Roles and Responsibilities*
- Crisis management team
- Spokesperson designation
- Decision authority matrix
- Backup personnel
- External resources

*Communication Protocols*
- Internal notification chain
- External communication approval
- Media response procedures
- Social media protocols
- Stakeholder priority matrix

**Stakeholder Priority Matrix**

*Tier 1 - Immediate Notification*
- Board of Directors
- Executive team

**Communication Channels by Stakeholder**

### 4. Message Development

**Core Message Framework**
- What happened (facts only)
- What we're doing about it
- What stakeholders should do
- Where to get more information
- Expression of appropriate concern

**Message Components**

*Initial Statement*
- Acknowledge situation
- Express appropriate concern
- State commitment to address
- Promise updates
- Provide contact information

*Detailed Statement*
- Full situation explanation
- Timeline of events
- Actions being taken
- Impact mitigation
- Ongoing commitment

*Updates*
- Progress on resolution
- New information
- Revised guidance
- Continued commitment

**Tone Guidelines**
- Empathetic but professional
- Factual, avoid speculation
- Take appropriate responsibility
- Forward-looking focus
- Consistent across channels
