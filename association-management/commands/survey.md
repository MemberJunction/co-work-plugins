# /survey

Design effective surveys for member feedback, research, and assessment.

## Usage

```
/survey [survey-type] [options]
```

## Arguments

- `survey-type`: Type of survey (member-satisfaction, needs-assessment, event-feedback, exit, pulse, research, compensation)
- `--audience`: Target respondent group
- `--length`: Target completion time (quick <5min, standard 5-10min, comprehensive 15+min)
- `--frequency`: One-time, annual, recurring
- `--goals`: Primary research objectives

## What This Command Does

Create scientifically-sound survey instruments:

**Research Objectives**
- Primary questions to answer
- Decision points survey will inform

- Action planning connection

**Methodology Selection**
- Quantitative vs. qualitative
- Cross-sectional vs. longitudinal
- Census vs. sample
- Anonymous vs. identified

**Survey Specifications**
- Target completion time
- Question count limits
- Device optimization
- Accessibility requirements
- Language considerations

### 3. Question Design

**Question Types**

*Scaled Questions*
- Likert scales (agreement)
- Satisfaction scales
- Importance scales
- Frequency scales
- Net Promoter Score (0-10)

*Multiple Choice*
- Single select
- Multi-select
- Ranking/prioritization
- Forced choice

*Open-Ended*
- Short text
- Long text
- Comment boxes
- Follow-up probes

*Matrix Questions*
- Rating grids
- Evaluation matrices
- Importance/satisfaction gaps

**Question Quality Checklist**
- Single concept per question
- Neutral wording (no leading)
- Appropriate scale anchors
- Exhaustive response options
- Mutually exclusive choices
- "Don't know/NA" option when appropriate
- Clear, jargon-free language
- Logical flow and grouping

### 8. Common Pitfalls to Avoid

**Design Errors**
- Double-barreled questions
- Leading questions
- Biased response options
- Survey fatigue (too long)
- Missing options
- Unclear instructions

**Administration Errors**
- Poor timing
- Inadequate promotion
- No mobile optimization
- Broken links
- No reminder strategy

**Analysis Errors**
- Over-interpretation
- Ignoring non-response bias
- Cherry-picking results
- Missing context
- No action planning

Creates a comprehensive annual member satisfaction survey with benchmarking capabilities.
