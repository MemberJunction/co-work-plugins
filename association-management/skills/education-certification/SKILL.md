## Adult Learning Principles

### Andragogy (Adult Learning Theory)
Adults learn differently than children. Key principles:

**Self-Direction**
- Adults want control over learning
- Provide choices and flexibility
- Support self-paced options

**Experience-Based**
- Adults bring rich experience
- Connect new learning to existing knowledge
- Use case studies and examples
- Facilitate peer learning

**Relevance**
- Adults need to see immediate application
- Focus on practical, job-relevant content
- Address real-world problems
- Provide tools they can use

**Problem-Centered**
- Adults prefer problem-solving approaches
- Use scenarios and simulations
- Focus on "how to" not just "what"

**Motivation**
- Adults are internally motivated
- Connect to career goals
- Recognize achievement
- Provide credentials and recognition

### Learning Objectives
Use Bloom's Taxonomy levels:
1. **Remember** - Recall facts and concepts
2. **Understand** - Explain ideas
3. **Apply** - Use information in new situations
4. **Analyze** - Draw connections
5. **Evaluate** - Justify decisions
6. **Create** - Produce new work

### Assessment of Learning
- **Level 1 - Reaction**: Did they like it?
- **Level 2 - Learning**: Did they learn?
- **Level 3 - Behavior**: Did they apply it?
- **Level 4 - Results**: Did it impact outcomes?
- **Level 5 - ROI**: What was the return?

## Certification Programs

### Certification vs. Certificate

**Certification**
- Validates competency against standards
- Requires examination
- Ongoing maintenance (recertification)
- Industry-recognized credential
- Typically accredited

**Certificate**
- Recognizes program completion
- No examination required
- No maintenance required
- Training acknowledgment
- Provider-specific

### Certification Development

**Job Task Analysis (JTA)**
- Define scope of practice
- Identify critical tasks
- Determine knowledge/skills required
- Validate with practitioners
- Create exam blueprint

**Examination Development**
- Item writing (multiple SMEs)
- Item review and refinement
- Pilot testing
- Psychometric analysis
- Cut score setting
- Security protocols

**Eligibility Requirements**
- Education requirements
- Experience requirements
- Professional references
- Ethics/conduct agreements
- Application process

**Recertification**
- Continuing education requirements
- Re-examination options
- Activity-based maintenance
- Cycle duration (typically 2-3 years)

### Certification Accreditation

**ANSI/ISO 17024**
- International standard for certification bodies
- Impartiality requirements
- Competence validation
- Examination standards
- Governance requirements

**ICE (Institute for Credentialing Excellence)**
- NCCA accreditation
- Industry-recognized standard
- Comprehensive standards
- Peer review process

**Benefits of Accreditation**
- Third-party validation
- Quality assurance
- Market credibility
- Legal defensibility
- Continuous improvement
