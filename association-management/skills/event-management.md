# Event Management

This skill provides comprehensive knowledge for planning and executing successful association events from small meetings to large conferences.

## Event Strategy

### Events in Association Context
Events serve multiple purposes for associations:
- Revenue generation (often largest non-dues source)
- Member engagement and retention
- Professional development delivery
- Networking facilitation
- Industry visibility
- Non-member recruitment
- Sponsor/exhibitor relationships

### Event Portfolio Strategy
- Annual conference (flagship)
- Regional meetings
- Educational workshops
- Networking events
- Virtual programs
- Chapter events
- Special events (galas, awards)

### Event Success Metrics
- Attendance and growth
- Revenue and margin
- Attendee satisfaction
- Learning outcomes
- Networking effectiveness
- Sponsor/exhibitor satisfaction
- Lead generation

## Event Planning Process

### 12-Month Planning Timeline (Large Events)

**12+ Months Out**
- Date selection and venue contracting
- Budget development
- Theme/branding concepts
- Key speaker identification
- Sponsor prospectus development

**9 Months Out**
- Call for proposals
- Speaker recruitment
- Marketing plan finalization
- Registration system setup
- Exhibit sales begin

**6 Months Out**
- Program content finalization
- Early bird registration opens
- Sponsor fulfillment begins
- Logistics planning
- Vendor contracting

**3 Months Out**
- Full marketing push
- Speaker confirmations
- Registration momentum building
- Signage and materials production
- Mobile app development

**1 Month Out**
- Final logistics
- On-site staff assignments
- Speaker rehearsals
- Material shipping
- Final registration push

**Week Of**
- Setup and rehearsals
- Staff briefings
- Real-time communications
- On-site registration
- Program execution

**Post-Event**
- Survey distribution
- Financial reconciliation
- Debrief and lessons learned
- Thank you communications
- Next year planning begins

## Venue Selection

### Venue Types
- Convention centers (large events)
- Hotels (built-in lodging)
- Conference centers (dedicated meeting space)
- Unique venues (museums, historic sites)
- Academic facilities
- Hybrid-ready facilities

### Selection Criteria
- Capacity and room configuration
- Location accessibility
- Cost and value
- Sleeping room availability
- Technology infrastructure
- Food and beverage quality
- Accessibility compliance
- Sustainability practices
- Union vs. non-union considerations

### Contract Negotiation
- Room rates and concessions
- Attrition clauses
- Cancellation terms
- Force majeure provisions
- Complimentary rooms
- Meeting room rental
- Food and beverage minimums
- Exclusivity requirements
