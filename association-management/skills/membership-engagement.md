# Membership Engagement

This skill provides comprehensive strategies for member recruitment, retention, and engagement throughout the membership lifecycle.

## Membership Value Proposition

### Defining Member Value
Value is the perceived benefits relative to the cost (dues, time, attention). Different members value different things:

**Career Advancement**
- Professional development
- Certification/credentials
- Job opportunities
- Skill development
- Leadership opportunities

**Knowledge and Information**
- Industry intelligence
- Best practices
- Research and data
- Publications
- Expert access

**Networking and Community**
- Peer connections
- Mentorship
- Collaboration opportunities
- Social belonging
- Professional identity

**Advocacy and Representation**
- Voice in policy
- Industry promotion
- Standards development
- Collective influence

**Business Benefits**
- Leads and referrals
- Discounts and savings
- Visibility and recognition
- Tools and resources

### Value Proposition Communication
- Articulate benefits clearly
- Quantify value when possible (ROI)
- Tailor to different segments
- Demonstrate ongoing value
- Use member testimonials

## Membership Models

### Individual Membership
- Person-based membership
- Direct relationship
- Individual dues payment
- Full participation rights
- Common in professional societies

### Organizational Membership
- Company/institution-based
- Primary + designated representatives
- Tiered by organization size
- Shared benefits among reps
- Common in trade associations

### Hybrid Models
- Both individual and organizational
- Different benefit structures
- Cross-selling opportunities
- Complex administration

### Tiered Membership
- Multiple levels (e.g., Basic, Professional, Premium)
- Graduated benefits
- Price differentiation
- Upgrade path
- Value ladder concept

### Freemium/Community Models
- Free basic access
- Paid premium membership
- Conversion funnel approach
- Lower barrier to entry
- Emerging model

## Member Onboarding

### Critical First 90 Days
The onboarding period sets the trajectory for long-term engagement and retention.

**Week 1: Welcome**
- Immediate confirmation
- Welcome package/email
- Login credentials
- Quick start guide
- Personal welcome (phone/video for high-value)

**Month 1: Orientation**
- Benefits overview
- Portal walkthrough
- Community introduction
- First engagement opportunity
- Early value demonstration

**Month 2-3: Integration**
- Deeper engagement opportunities
- Committee/volunteer invitation
- Mentorship connection
- Event participation
- Feedback solicitation

### Onboarding Content

- New member guide
- Video orientations
- Personalized recommendations
- FAQ and support resources

### Onboarding Metrics
- Email open rates
- Portal login rates
- First event attendance
- Resource utilization
- Early renewal indicators

## Member Engagement

### Engagement Definition
Engagement is the depth and breadth of a member's interaction with and commitment to the organization.

### Engagement Ladder
1. **Aware** - Knows about organization
2. **Member** - Joined but passive
3. **Participant** - Attends events, consumes content
4. **Contributor** - Volunteers, shares expertise
5. **Leader** - Committee chair, board member
6. **Ambassador** - Actively promotes organization

### Engagement Drivers

**Relevance**
- Content matches needs
- Services solve problems
- Timing is right
- Personalization

**Connection**
- Peer relationships

- Community belonging
- Shared identity

**Recognition**
- Appreciation for contributions
- Awards and honors
- Visibility opportunities
- Achievement celebration

**Impact**
- Voice is heard
- Contributions matter
- Industry influence
- Professional growth

## Member Retention

### Retention Fundamentals
- Retention is more cost-effective than recruitment (5-7x)
- Most attrition happens in years 1-2
- Engaged members renew at higher rates
- At-risk identification enables intervention

### Retention Metrics
- Overall retention rate (target: 85%+)
- First-year retention (often 10-15% lower)
- Retention by segment
- Lifetime value
- Churn analysis

### Retention Formula
Retention Rate = (Members at End - New Members) / Members at Start × 100

### Retention Strategies

**Value Reinforcement**
- Regular value reminders
- Utilization reporting
- ROI demonstrations
- Success stories
- Exclusive content

**Renewal Process**
- Multiple touches (6-8 minimum)
- Multi-channel approach
- Easy renewal process
- Auto-renewal option
- Early renewal incentives

**At-Risk Intervention**
- Engagement monitoring
- Proactive outreach
- Personalized retention offers
- Exit prevention
- Save strategies

**Win-Back Programs**
- Lapsed member campaigns
- Re-engagement offers
- "We miss you" outreach
- Updated value proposition
- Feedback incorporation

### Retention Timeline
- 90 days pre-expiration: Value summary
- 60 days: First renewal notice
- 30 days: Reminder with urgency
- 15 days: Final notice
- Expiration: Grace period offer
- 30 days post: Win-back attempt

- What needs are unmet?
- How can we improve?

### Net Promoter Score (NPS)
"How likely are you to recommend [organization] to a colleague?"
- Promoters (9-10): Loyal enthusiasts
- Passives (7-8): Satisfied but vulnerable
- Detractors (0-6): Unhappy, risk of churn
- NPS = % Promoters - % Detractors
