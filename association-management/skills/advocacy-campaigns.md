# Advocacy Campaigns

This skill provides comprehensive knowledge for planning and executing effective advocacy and government relations programs.

## Advocacy Fundamentals

### What is Association Advocacy?
Advocacy is the act of supporting or recommending a cause, policy, or course of action on behalf of members and the industry/profession. For associations, it includes:

- Legislative engagement (bills and laws)
- Regulatory affairs (rules and regulations)
- Grassroots mobilization (member activation)
- Coalition building (allied organizations)
- Public affairs (shaping public opinion)
- Political activity (where permitted)

### Why Associations Advocate
- Protect member interests
- Shape favorable policy environment
- Advance industry/profession
- Provide collective voice
- Demonstrate member value
- Fulfill organizational mission

### Legal Framework

**501(c)(6) Organizations**
- Lobbying permitted as primary activity
- Must serve common business interest
- Political activity limited (not primary purpose)
- Dues may have lobbying disclosure implications

**501(c)(3) Organizations**
- Lobbying limited (typically <5-20% of budget)
- Insubstantial part test or 501(h) election
- No political campaign intervention
- Educational activities permitted

**501(c)(4) Organizations**
- Substantial lobbying permitted
- Can engage in political activity (not primary)
- Social welfare purpose required

## Government Relations

### Legislative Process Knowledge

**Federal Level**
- Bill introduction and referral
- Committee consideration
- Floor action
- Conference committees
- Presidential action

**State Level**
- Similar process, faster timeline
- Part-time vs. full-time legislatures
- Session schedules
- State-specific rules

**Local Level**
- City councils, county boards
- Zoning and regulatory bodies
- School boards
- Special districts

### Regulatory Process

**Federal Rulemaking**
- Proposed rule publication
- Comment period (typically 30-90 days)
- Final rule publication
- Implementation timeline
- Judicial review possibility

**State and Local**
- Agency-specific procedures
- Licensing boards
- Permit processes
- Administrative hearings

### Relationship Building

**With Legislators**
- Become a trusted resource
- Provide expertise and data
- Be responsive and reliable
- Support when possible
- Maintain relationships between asks

**With Staff**
- Staff often drive policy details
- Build relationships with key aides
- Provide concise, useful information
- Respect their time

**With Agencies**
- Engage proactively, not just reactively
- Participate in rulemaking
- Serve on advisory committees
- Provide technical expertise

## Advocacy Strategy

### Issue Identification
- Monitor legislative and regulatory activity
- Assess member impact
- Evaluate organizational capacity
- Prioritize based on:
  - Impact magnitude
  - Likelihood of influence
  - Resource requirements
  - Member interest

### Position Development
- Research and analysis
- Member input
- Expert consultation
- Board/committee approval
- Documentation and communication

### Strategy Selection

**Offensive Strategies** (promoting change)
- Champion legislation
- Propose regulatory changes
- Build coalitions
- Public campaigns
- Member mobilization

**Defensive Strategies** (preventing harm)
- Opposition campaigns
- Amendment efforts
- Coalition opposition
- Grassroots opposition
- Legal challenges

**Neutral/Monitoring**
- Track developments
- Maintain relationships
- Preserve options
- Gather information

## Lobbying

### Direct Lobbying
Communication with legislators or staff about specific legislation:
- Meetings and testimony
- Written communications
- Phone calls
- Formal testimony
- Technical assistance

### Grassroots Lobbying
Communication with the public encouraging them to contact legislators:
- Action alerts
- Social media campaigns
- Town halls
- Constituent meetings
- Letter-writing campaigns

### Effective Lobbying Practices

**Preparation**
- Know the issue thoroughly
- Understand legislator's position
- Anticipate questions/objections
- Prepare concise talking points
- Bring supporting materials

**The Ask**
- Be clear and specific
- Explain member impact
- Provide supporting data
- Offer to be a resource
- Follow up appropriately

**Ongoing Engagement**
- Express gratitude
- Provide updates
- Remain a resource
- Maintain relationship
