# Volunteer Engagement

This skill provides comprehensive strategies for recruiting, managing, and retaining volunteers who are essential to association success.

## Volunteer Value in Associations

### Why Volunteers Matter
- Extend organizational capacity
- Provide expertise and perspective
- Build member engagement
- Develop future leaders
- Reduce operational costs
- Enhance credibility and legitimacy
- Strengthen member loyalty

### Types of Association Volunteers

**Governance Volunteers**
- Board of Directors
- Committee chairs
- Task force leaders
- Advisory council members

**Program Volunteers**
- Committee members
- Conference speakers
- Content reviewers
- Mentors and coaches

**Event Volunteers**
- Conference support
- Registration assistance
- Session moderators
- Local hosts

**Subject Matter Experts**
- Standards developers
- Exam item writers
- Publication reviewers
- Research contributors

**Ambassadors**
- Member recruiters
- Advocacy champions
- Social media advocates
- Community builders
