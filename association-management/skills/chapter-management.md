# Chapter Management

This skill provides comprehensive knowledge for managing geographic chapters, sections, and local affiliate organizations.

## Chapter Structures

### What Are Chapters?
Chapters are geographically-based sub-units of a national or international association that provide:
- Local presence and engagement
- Member networking opportunities
- Regional programming
- Grassroots advocacy
- Leadership development
- Community building

### Organizational Models

**Integrated/Subordinate Chapters**
- Part of parent organization's legal structure
- Operate under parent's tax exemption
- Governed by parent policies
- Financial oversight by parent
- Most common model

**Affiliated/Independent Chapters**
- Separate legal entities
- Own tax exemption
- Affiliation agreement with parent
- More autonomy, less control
- Shared branding and mission

**Hybrid Models**
- Mix of integrated and affiliated
- Varies by state/region
- Historical evolution
- Transition considerations

### Chapter vs. Other Components

**Sections/Divisions**
- Interest-based, not geographic
- Specialty areas or practice types
- May overlap with chapters
- Often virtual operation

**Regions**
- Groupings of chapters
- Coordination and support
- Regional meetings
- Leadership development

**Student Chapters**
- University-based
- Pipeline development
- Special governance considerations
- Faculty advisor model

## Chapter Financial Management

### Financial Models

**Centralized Model**
- Parent holds all funds
- Chapters submit expenses
- Standardized operations
- Maximum control
- Less chapter autonomy

**Distributed Model**
- Chapters maintain own accounts
- Keep event revenue
- Manage own expenses
- More autonomy
- Harder to monitor

**Hybrid Model**
- Shared revenue arrangements
- Expense reimbursement
- Reserve requirements
- Most common approach

### Chapter Revenue Sources
- Dues allocation
- Event registration
- Sponsorships
- Educational programs
- Fundraising activities

### Financial Oversight
- Budget requirements
- Reporting frequency
- Audit procedures
- Reserve policies
- Spending guidelines

### Financial Best Practices
- Segregation of duties
- Two-signature requirements
- Regular reconciliation
- Timely reporting
- Transparent practices
